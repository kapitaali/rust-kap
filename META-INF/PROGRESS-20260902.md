## What landed
Two independent bug fixups, both real (not display) bugs found by comparing the parser's `is_primitive_op` against the evaluator's `is_primitive_name`:

### 1. Parser gate missing 5 glyphs
`~` (not), `∊` (membership), `⍋` (grade-up), `⊤` (encode), `⊥` (decode) were in the evaluator's `is_primitive_name` (the eval-time late gate) but **not** in the parser's `is_primitive_op`. The parser's symbol dispatch checks `is_primitive_op(qual)` to decide whether to route a symbol through `finish_fn_call` (function application) or strand it as a value. Missing glyphs stranded → `~ 0` became a bare function value → "No arguments specified for function", `~¨ 1 0` hit the adverb-without-left-fn guard, `⍋ 3 1 4 1 5` and `2 9 4 ∊ 1 2 3 4` errored "No arguments specified for function", `2 2 2 ⊤ 5` stranded into `(2 2 2 <function> 5)`.

**Fix:** Added `"~" | "∊" | "⍋" | "⊤" | "⊥"` to the `is_primitive_op` match arm at `kap-core/src/parser.rs:3266`.

### 2. Transpose rank check misplaced (commit `935bf93` regression)
`groups.len() != src_dims.len()` was placed in `transpose_diagonal` (forward path), but Kotlin performs this check only in `InverseDiagonalTransposedValue` (inverse path, `transpose.kt:428`). The forward path `TransposedDiagonalValue` (`:376`) has **no** such check. This made `0 0 ⍉ 4 4 ⍴ ⍳16` (2 groups vs rank-2 — valid forward diagonal) wrongly error with "Number of axis in left argument must match...", while `0 0⍉˝2 3⍴...` (inverse path; 1 group vs rank-2 — genuinely invalid) wrongly succeeded.

**Fix:** Removed the check from `transpose_diagonal` (`evaluator.rs`) and added an equivalent `distinct.len() != src_dims.len()` check to `inverse_diagonal` (`evaluator.rs:6738-6746`), matching Kotlin's exact placement.
