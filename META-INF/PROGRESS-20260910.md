1. `⍞(` over-consumed past `)` in nested positions: `parse_function_atom`'s
   `⍞(` arm (parser.rs:5846) and the legacy-primary `⍞(` arm (parser.rs:6570)
   called `parse_value_kotlin` WITHOUT pushing `CloseParen` to
   `kotlin_close_stack` — the main-accumulator `⍞(` arm does push it
   (parser.rs:776). `λ(⍞(i⊃(λ+)(λ÷)))` misparsed the right operand → ⊃
   "Mismatched dimensions". Fix: push/pop `CloseParen` in both arms.
2. `λ(⍞ref)` late binding: port evaluated the ref at CALL time in the stale
   scope (both lambdas read i=2 → OOB). Kotlin `computeClosure`
   (instr.kt:222-230) + `EvalLambdaFnx` (instr.kt:564-570) snapshot the ref into
   a per-creation anonymous var (`createAnonymousSymbol`, engine.kt:754).
   Fix: `Lambda` eval arm snapshots `DynamicRefExpr`/`DynamicRef` into
   `"<anonymous applyRef N>"` (fresh `Engine::anon_syms` counter, unutterable
   in source) in the creation env; closure body reads it via `DynamicRef`
   (already in the apply_user_fn re-dispatch list).
